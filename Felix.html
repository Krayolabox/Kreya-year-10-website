Roles in stray kids: Lead Dancer, Sub-rapper, and vocalist.

Story: Felix was born and raised in Sydney, Australia, and is a standout member of DanceRacha. He joined JYPE in 2017 He is most famous for his deep, gravelly bass voice, which provides some of Stray Kids' most iconic and viral lines. Despite his "tough" vocal sound, he is known as the group's "Sunshine" due to his pure heart, bright smile, and warm personality. He is a talented dancer with incredible charisma and is often recognized for his unique, ethereal visuals and natural freckles. In his free time, he loves baking brownies for the members and staff, and he is a passionate gamer. Though he moved to Korea with limited language skills, he worked tirelessly to become fluent, proving his dedication to the group.

Facts: Felix is known as the "Sunshine" of the group. He has two sisters Rachael Lee and Olivia Lee. He loves to bake mostly brownies. He is a Global Ambassador for Louis Vuitton and has even walked the runway at Paris Fashion Week.
