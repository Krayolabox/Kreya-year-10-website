CHANGBIN

credited to: 티비텐

Name: Seo Chang-bin (3racha name: SPEARB).

Birthday: 11 August 1999

Age: 26 years old

MBTI: ESTP

SKZOO: Dwaekki

Roles in Stray Kids: Main rapper, songwriter, producer.

Rachas: 3RACHA, and GymRacha.

Story: Changbin was born in Yongin, South Korea, and is a core member of the production trio 3RACHA. He joined JYPE in 2016 Known for his "Jwaegyo" (a mix of his name and aegyo), he balances a fierce stage presence with a playful, affectionate personality. He is the group's "muscle," famous for his dedication to the gym and his "Dwaekki" (pig-rabbit) SKZOO character. Above all, he is known for his unwavering confidence and for writing lyrics that encourage others to trust themselves and stay true to their path.

Facts: Changbin is known for being the king of aegyo (acting cute), being very loud, and for having the most confidence in Stray Kids, he is also Stray Kids GymBro. Before debuting, he competed on Show Me The Money 5, gaining respect for his powerful, "dark" rap style and lightning-fast delivery. His SKZOO's name comes from the combination for the korean names for rabbit and pig. He also has an older sister.
