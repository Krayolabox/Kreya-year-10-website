LEE KNOW

credited to: 티비텐

Name: Lee Min-ho AKA Lee Know

Birthday: 25 October 1998

Age: 27 years old

SKZOO: Leebit

Roles in Stray Kids: Main Dancer, and Vocalist.

Rachas: DanceRacha, PaboRacha, NyangnyangRacha, and Menace family-Racha.

Lee Know was born in Gimpo, South Korea, and is the group's second-oldest member. He joined JYPE in 2017, despite having one of the shortest training periods, he became the leader of DanceRacha, where he now polishes the group’s choreography with his "dancing gem" precision. He is famous for his "4D" personality—being unpredictable and mischievous. Though he acts blunt, he is known as the group's "hidden caregiver," quietly looking after the members' well-being.
