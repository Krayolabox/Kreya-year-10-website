Story: Seungmin was born in Seoul, South Korea, and is the group’s "Main Vocalist" and a member of VocalRacha. Known for his crystal-clear tone and stable live vocals, he is the group's "Dandy Boy" who constantly works to improve his craft. Before joining JYPE, he dreamed of being a baseball player, but he eventually followed his passion for music, joining the company in 2017 after winning second place in an open audition. He is famous for his "Savage" wit and his playful teasing of the older members, especially Bang Chan and Lee Know. Despite his mischievous side, he is incredibly disciplined, maintaining a daily diary and practicing relentlessly.
