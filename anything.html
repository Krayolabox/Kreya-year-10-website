WHAT IS STRAY KIDS??

Go Back

Stray Kids is an eight-member South Korean boy group formed by JYP Entertainment. They debuted in 2018 through a survival show with the same name, the group consists of eight members Bang Chan, Lee Know, Changbin, Hyunjin, HAN, Felix, Seungmin, and I.N. Usually a K-pop group sings, dances, and performs but they don't produce their own songs but Stray Kids is different, before Stray Kids was formed and sub-unit was formed with Bang Chan, Changbin, and HAN called 3RACHA this sub-unit was formed in 2017 and every since then they have been producing Stray Kids songs. They’re known for their song “God’s Menu” because that's the song that really brought them to their prime and because JYPE is usually know for forming groups which have a concept which is “Girl Crush” or “Refreshing Bubblegum Pop” but Stray kids is way different from that because their concept is “Noise Music” as they would call it and being out of the ordinary because the tracks feature heavy beats, experimental drums, and "industrial" sounds. And as every music group or artist does they have a fandom name which is “STAY” The group's motto is "Stray Kids everywhere all around the world," and the fandom name completes it: "You make Stray Kids stay."

WHAT ARE SKZOO'S?

In short, SKZOO is the ultimate blend of K-pop and mascot culture. Each character acts as an animated alter-ego for a Stray Kids member, turning their unique features and inside jokes into cute, collectible animals. There are eight SKZOO’S for all the eight members and they are Wolf Chan which is a wolf, Leebit which is a rabbit, Dwaekki which is a Pig and bunny hybrid, Jiniret which is a ferret, Han Quokka which is a Quokka, BbokAri which is a chick, PuppyM which is a puppy, Foxl.Ny which is a fennec fox.

WHAT ARE RACHA'S?

Rachas are group-units within Stray Kids. The three main Rachas are 3RACHA, DanceRACHA, and VocalRACHA.
